# Canal SSR coverage audit

Checked 4 October 2026 against public.ssr_item in Supabase project hqddsxnykndgcmxwqwmn and the current local canal components, quantity resolvers and estimate synchronization.

The CAW catalogue contains 110 items across chapters 1–8. A catalogue entry or generic item picker alone is not counted as a dedicated engineering workflow. Existing functionality was inspected, not revalidated against every SSR provision.

Bank Protection now covers CAW 8 stone pitching and grass turfing on outer bank slopes, with linked or independent bank tiers. Geometry, code resolution, persistence and estimate synchronization have automated tests.

## Main gaps

- CAW 6-1–2: rockfill casing from quarry or spoil/dump yard, distinct from the existing rock toe.
- CAW 7-4, 7-9, 7-18: bed-level stones, separately payable obstruction paver shifts, precast RCC templates. The legacy worksheet engine has these; the current Chapters 1–4 do not expose them or include legacy works in their billing branch.
- CAW 1-8–12: selectable manually in Earthwork, but no dedicated normal/controlled/smooth blasting decision or 1 m side-strip quantity separation. Basic hard-rock default is CAW 1-7.
- Field-channel-specific CAW 1-2, 1-5, 4-6 and 7-30 need dedicated workflows if field channels are included. CAW 1-3 is tank-bed desilting, not a normal canal item.

## Related PMW gaps

Site clearance currently measures one selected area item; separate counted trees/stumps/bamboo and girth-based additions are not exposed (PMW 1-3–15).

Investigation inputs record strata but do not estimate investigation work: trial pits, electrical resistivity stages and drilling (PMW 2-1–9). Boundary/chainage stones and temporary/permanent benchmarks (PMW 2-10–12) also lack dedicated schedules.

For repair canals, dedicated workflows remain for dry/slushy desilting (PMW 3-15–16), rain-cut repairs (3-12), disturbed slab/pitching resetting (3-4–5), rock-toe/revetment removal and rebuilding (3-1–3), other repair bank source variants (3-10–11, 3-19), and aquatic weed removal (3-20 variants). Generic selection of an excavation or area item does not supply these distinct measurement questions.

Road pavement uses the separate SSR/SOR picker; this audit does not treat CAW as the complete catalogue for roads or cross-drainage structures.
