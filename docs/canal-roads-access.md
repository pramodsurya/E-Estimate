# Roads & Access and canal chapters

Road platforms are an overlay on the saved bank design at the selected chainages and banks. The platform width includes the carriageway and both shoulders. At bank-top level it widens the crest only as needed. At a manual level it widens a matching outer berm or adds a shelf. Tiered banks apply the same rule: an inserted shelf splits the existing vertical drop without moving the original lower berms or changing their slopes. A left-only road does not widen the right bank, even with linked base tiers. Saved bank tier rules remain unchanged.

Sections, bank formation, foundation footprints, automatic embedded drainage widths, stripping, automatic jungle clearance and land acquisition use the shared revised profile. Hearting uses the effective crest width. Road formation is included in bank quantities once; cutting benches enter the shared excavation quantities once; Roads & Access measures pavement separately. The inner hydraulic opening and lining choices remain unchanged by an outer-bank road platform.

Road cards provide a section preview and identify measured sections where the selected bank has no usable shelf. Cutting sections create a road bench at TBL or a specified higher level, even when automatic cutting berms are disabled. Matching cutting benches widen to the maximum required width; road shelves preserve the selected cutting slopes. Higher manual levels are not clamped to TBL and do not widen embankment crests. A bench above natural ground, below TBL in cutting, or truncated by ground does not produce a full road platform. Road surfacing quantities are based on entered road dimensions and do not by themselves establish platform suitability. The warning leaves the user able to continue designing.

Regression coverage in `scripts/test-canal.cjs` checks tiered crest widening, independent banks, foundation and land-width effects, manual shelf insertion and matching, unchanged saved tiers, and chainage scope.

## Hearting geometry

When continuous cores meet within the embankment and both bank cutoffs are provided, the cutoff is one connected trench across their full span. The connection includes any space between the original bank trenches. Outside trench faces retain their entered slopes and depth; the intermediate prepared plane connects the two bank levels. The section diagram, excavation quantity and CNS excavation exclusions use this same connected polygon. Separate mode retains separate bank trenches.

Bank Design provides an explicit separate/continuous core connection choice, persisted in the design. Requested core outlines preserve the entered top width and side slopes rather than clamping their toes to separate bank footprints. The section preview shows these outlines dashed and shades the physical intersection with the solid fill envelope. Exact disjoint polygon cells count overlapping soil once and feed hearting/casing and CNS replacement quantities. Continuous mode permits overlap below the canal bed; it does not fill the canal opening or invent a connector where the cores do not meet. Separate overlap and core intrusion above the design surface are visible conflicts; hearting/casing quantities at those sections and their cutoff excavation remain pending. Cutoff trench quantities use a union too, avoiding duplicate excavation in overlapping trenches.
